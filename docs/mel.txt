Melanoma is a highly aggressive malignant tumor of melanocytes.

Clinical warning signs include:
- Asymmetry
- Irregular borders
- Multiple or uneven colors
- Diameter greater than 6 mm
- Rapid evolution

Dermoscopy features may include:
- Atypical pigment network
- Blue-white veil
- Irregular dots and globules
- Regression structures

Melanoma carries a high risk of metastasis and requires prompt diagnosis and surgical management.

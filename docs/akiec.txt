Actinic keratosis (AKIEC) is a premalignant skin lesion caused by long-term ultraviolet exposure.

Typical clinical presentation includes:
- Rough, scaly patches on sun-exposed areas
- Lesions may be erythematous or skin-colored
- Commonly found on the face, scalp, ears, and forearms

Dermoscopy features often include:
- Strawberry pattern
- White scales
- Red pseudonetwork

AKIEC has potential to progress to squamous cell carcinoma if untreated.

Common management strategies include:
- Cryotherapy
- Topical treatments
- Regular dermatologic follow-up

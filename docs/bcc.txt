Basal cell carcinoma (BCC) is the most common type of skin cancer.

Clinical features include:
- Pearly or translucent papules
- Rolled borders
- Telangiectasia
- Central ulceration in advanced cases

Dermoscopy findings:
- Arborizing vessels
- Blue-gray ovoid nests
- Absence of pigment network

BCC typically grows slowly and rarely metastasizes but can cause significant local tissue destruction.

Treatment options include surgical excision, Mohs surgery, and topical therapies for superficial lesions.

Differentiating melanoma from benign nevus is a critical task in dermatology.

Melanoma is more likely when lesions show:
- Asymmetry
- Irregular borders
- Color variegation
- Rapid change

Benign nevi usually present:
- Symmetric shape
- Uniform color
- Stable appearance

Basal cell carcinoma often presents as pearly papules with arborizing vessels, while actinic keratosis typically appears as rough, scaly lesions on sun-exposed skin.
